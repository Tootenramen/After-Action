    How to hold space for a teammate
    
        
      
          
    • Ask, then be quiet."How are you, after that?" Then wait. Silence is part of the answer.
      • 
          
    • Thank them specifically.Name the exact thing they did at 3 a.m. Vague praise slides off.
      • 
          
    • Offer cover, not advice."I've got the pager tomorrow morning" beats any tip about resilience.
      • 
          
    • Don't make them retell it.The timeline is in the review. This is about the person.
      • 
          
    • Normalize the crash.Being flat or wired for a couple of days afterwards is common. Say so.
      • 
          
    • Follow up in two days.One short message. No agenda. It matters more than the meeting.
      •
